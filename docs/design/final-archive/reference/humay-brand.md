# Humay Brand

**Purpose:** Consumer brand identity — name, wordmark, occultation mark, app icon, palette/type system, launch treatment, motion and misuse rules.

**Current authority:** Pass 5.1 — **Humay Brand Lock** — is the brand authority. Pass 5 remains historical exploration only and is superseded wherever it conflicts with Pass 5.1.

**Source artifact:** `Product Design - Pass 5.1 - Humay Brand Lock.dc.html`.

**Final decisions:**
- **Humay** (preferred pronunciation: hoo-MAY) is the selected consumer-facing brand; canonical wordmark is **HUMAY**.
- **Eclipse** is the visual direction only, never a product name.
- **Astara** is the repository/project working name only, never consumer-facing.
- The occultation mark is the primary brand symbol; no bird, feather, wing, phoenix, crown or literal mythological imagery is introduced.
- Historical/cultural inspiration gives the name depth; it is never presented as proof, luck, destiny, divine favor or a fortune guarantee.
- External clearance remains required before public commitment where applicable.
