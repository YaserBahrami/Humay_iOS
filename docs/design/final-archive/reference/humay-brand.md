# Humay Brand

**Purpose:** Consumer brand identity — name, wordmark, mark, icon, palette/type, and launch treatment.

**Current authority:** Pass 5.1 ("Humay Brand Lock") is the brand authority in full. **Pass 5 is historical brand exploration only** and is superseded wherever it conflicts with Pass 5.1.

**Source artifact:** `Product Design - Pass 5.1 - Humay Brand Lock.dc.html`.

**Final decisions:**
- **Humay** (hoo-MAY) is the consumer-facing brand and wordmark **HUMAY**.
- **Eclipse** is visual direction only — never a user-facing name.
- **Astara** is a repo/project name only — never surfaced to users.
- The occultation mark is the primary brand symbol, used independently of the wordmark where space is constrained (e.g. app icon).
