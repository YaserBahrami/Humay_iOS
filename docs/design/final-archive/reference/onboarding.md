# Onboarding

**Purpose:** The approved onboarding flow from welcome and birth-data collection through calculation and the initial Big Three reveal. This archive contains representative captures rather than every intermediate state.

**Current authority:** `Onboarding.dc.html` is the dedicated onboarding visual reference. Pass 6.1 controls unknown-birth-time reliability/availability behavior wherever an older onboarding visual or copy is less precise.

**Source artifact:** `Onboarding.dc.html` (with earlier overlapping exploration retained historically in other passes).

**Final decisions:**
- Welcome/value framing begins on Ink; data-entry screens use Paper; calculation/reveal returns to the darker celestial surface language.
- Missing birth time is never treated as an invalid profile. The flow explains limitations and allows continuation without fabricated precision.
- Unknown-time effects are evaluated fact by fact using Pass 6.1's uncertainty rules; the app must not assume every non-house placement remains reliable.
- System selection sets the initial reading preference; Western, Vedic and Chinese/BaZi remain product systems rather than mutually exclusive account types.
