5

Two rewarded entitlement shapes, and only two

Adds to Pass 6 §10 — no reversal

A · Persistent content unlock

One identified reading/section. Once unlocked, stays unlocked. Survives restart. That item never asks again.

B · Immediate Ask extension

One completed ad permits one additional Ask question. Single-use — consumed on that question, not stored as a balance.

Neither shape generalizes into a visible, bankable coin/credit/token system — no stacking UI, no balance display, no "3 credits remaining." Every other rewarded surface in §10's table is shape A. Ask's "+1 question per ad" is shape B. There is no shape C.
