The first thing worth knowing

Your front door and your interior agree, loudly.

A Libra Rising smooths every edge in the room. An Aries Moon wants to move now and say so. They sit on the same axis, opposite signs pulling the same rope — composure on the surface, urgency right underneath it, with no real distance between them.

In practice: you rarely hide what you feel — you just translate it into something diplomatic before anyone notices the translation.

14 · First personalised insight

The first ink-to-paper boundary the user meets, and the template every insight in the product follows: statement on ink → practical meaning on paper → chart factors → an honest note about what kind of claim this is.

Motion

Screens advance with a 320ms horizontal push; the coordinate rail tick travels independently and lands 80ms late, so progress reads as a separate object from content. The reveal is the only sequence longer than half a second, and it can be skipped by tapping.

States not drawn

Location with no network (manual coordinate entry); a date before 1900 (ephemeris warning, still calculates); permission denied (returns to the flow silently, no second ask); resuming a half-finished onboarding from the welcome screen.

Android deltas

Back is a system gesture, so the ‹ chevron is dropped and the rail carries progress alone. Date and time use the Material picker rather than the wheel — same gilt selection band, no rewriting of native input. The notification permission screen moves after first use of Today, matching Android 13+ convention.
