Light Eclipse, Dark Eclipse

Ink and Paper are roles, not colours — so appearance preference never fights the system

Ink is the stage: the sky, the reveal, calculation, roast, share. Paper is the reading surface: interpretation, lists, forms, reports. That relationship holds in both appearances — what changes is which pigment each role is made of, and it is never an inversion.

Light Eclipse
PAPER · GROUND  #F4F1EA
PAPER · RAISED  #FBFAF6 + hairline .16
PAPER · RECESSED  #E9E4D8
INK · STAGE  #12151C — top-anchored band
GILT · TEXT  #8A6524  /  STROKE #B0863F

Ink arrives as a band — headers, reveals, plates. Paper carries the page. Chart lines are ink at 1.4px on paper; celestial geometry sits inside the ink band only. Roast turns the band's accent cinnabar and thickens its rules.

Dark Eclipse
INK · STAGE  #0A0C11 — deepest, celestial
INK · GROUND  #12151C
PAPER → SLATE · READING  #1B202A
SLATE · RECESSED  #232935
GILT · #D9AD63  /  CINNABAR #E0603E

Paper does not become white-on-black. It becomes Slate — a raised reading plane one step lighter than the ground, so the same "stage above, reading below" order survives. Hairlines go ivory .14, chart strokes gain +0.2px, and reading type drops to 88% ivory to hold Lora's colour.

System appearance

Follows iOS. Both appearances are authored, neither is derived. Settings offers Light · Dark · System only — no third "Ink mode", because Ink is not a theme.

Elevation

Light: elevation is a hairline and a shade lighter than ground. Dark: elevation is a step lighter in Slate. No shadows in either, except sheets, which get a single 24px ambient at 18%.

Charts & celestial lines

Wheel and horizon are ink-on-paper / ivory-on-ink at matched perceived weight. Celestial geometry — the eclipse arc, terminator, orbit rings — is only ever drawn on an Ink surface, in both appearances.

Roast

Light: cinnabar #C2472A on paper, ink stage unchanged. Dark: cinnabar lifts to #E0603E and the stage deepens to #0A0C11. Roast never flips the appearance — it sharpens the one you chose.

Frames below are labelled LIGHT or DARK. The Today screen is shown in both to prove the rule holds.

What Pass 2 settled
  • "Evidence" is retired in the interface. The user-facing phrase is What's influencing this; the section title is Astrological basis.
  • Light and Dark Eclipse are both authored, with Paper becoming Slate rather than inverting.
  • Compare Systems compares themes and emphasis. The consensus band is gone.
  • Times appear only for real astronomical events; everything else is a part of the day.
  • Five dimensions first, chosen by relationship type; the rest unfold.
Open, and worth deciding next
  • Vedic and Chinese need their own home screens — the Today template holds, but nakshatra/dasha and four-pillar layouts are their own pass.
  • Sky and Chart tabs are stubs here; the natal wheel needs its own interaction design.
  • Ask — the conversational layer — is referenced from three screens and not yet designed.
  • The product name. Ortus is a placeholder in every wordmark above.
