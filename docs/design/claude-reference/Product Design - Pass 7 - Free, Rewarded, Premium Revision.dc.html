The product rule under all of this: astrology calculations come from structured calculation data (placements, houses, aspects, timing). AI interprets those facts into voice — it never invents a placement, house, aspect or timing window that wasn't calculated. This is true at every tier; Free and Precise differ in which calculated facts exist (sign-level vs. birth-time-and-place), never in whether the astrology shown is real.

Astrological content, copy and figures shown in this pass are illustrative prototype content only, not calculation fixtures. Deterministic astrology engines and their validated outputs remain the source of truth.

01

Free is a full product. Precise is a deeper one.

Sign-based vs. birth-time-and-place — not "demo" vs. "real"

Free never shows a birth chart it can't calculate — no wheel, no houses, no aspects synthesized from a date alone. What Free shows instead is genuinely useful and genuinely finished: a Sun-sign Today across four life areas, a cosmic profile per tradition, sign-to-sign compatibility, and a curated Ask that rotates daily. All of it is pre-generated and cached, so it loads instantly and needs no personalized API or AI call. Precise readings — full natal chart, synastry, chart-based Ask — need birth time and place and are computed and interpreted per person. That's the line: Free is sign-level and cached; Precise is calculated and personal.

06

The gate shows before the answer, never after

Free: curated, cached, sign-level · Precise: open questions against your real chart

Free Ask offers six curated, pre-written questions for the user's sign each day, rotated from a larger cached pool — answered instantly, no runtime AI call, and no cap on how many of the six someone opens. The rotation is deterministic, not random: a stable key of date + sign + language + content version selects the six, so everyone with the same sign and language sees the same six that day, and reopening the app never reshuffles them mid-day. An open question needs the real chart, so it needs a runtime interpretation call, which is exactly the Rewarded/Premium boundary. The access decision always happens before generation starts: the unlock options sit where the answer would go, not after a result is produced and then hidden.

08

Premium sells depth, not the removal of a limit

Corrects the wordmark (was "Eclipse Premium") and the framing (was "none of the guesswork")

Free was never guesswork, so Premium can't be sold as fixing it. The pitch is standing personalization — precise Today every day, every chart, every pairing, without reaching for an ad each time. It states plainly that a reading already unlocked with an ad stays unlocked on any plan, and it never implies permanence: this is a subscription, framed as durable value, not a lifetime deal.
