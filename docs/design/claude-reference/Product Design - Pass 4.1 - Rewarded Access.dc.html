Rewarded access,
without becoming an ad economy

Pass 4 is unchanged. This pass adds one optional path for free users who don't want to subscribe: a voluntary rewarded ad that unlocks one bounded piece of premium depth. Free stays useful. Premium stays the best experience. Nothing autoplays, interrupts, or gates a user's own basic chart.

Everything below is additive to the approved Pass 4 architecture. No banner, native-feed, interstitial or launch ads appear anywhere. Ads are always rewarded, always voluntary, and the reward is always stated before the ad begins.

01

Free → Rewarded → Premium

A hierarchy, not a menu of equal options

Rewarded access sits strictly between Free and Premium. It never outranks Premium visually, and it never becomes the primary way the product monetizes. Four states cover every surface, and none of them borrow gamified language — no coins, gems, energy or streaks. This is an editorial product, not a mobile game.

Free Reward available Reward unlocked Premium
Rewarded ads never
  • Autoplay, or interrupt a reading in progress
  • Appear between normal screens as interstitials
  • Block basic navigation or a user's own natal basics
  • Appear in onboarding or privacy/data controls
  • Disguise themselves as application content
Every reward prompt states
  • Exactly what unlocks, before the ad starts
  • That the unlock is bounded — one section, one question, one reading
  • Premium as the equally-visible alternative, same weight

03

Compatibility

The strongest rewarded-access candidate

Overview, strongest connection, main friction and several primary dimensions stay free. Deeper sections — conflict pattern, communication dynamics, emotional needs, deeper synastry — sit behind the contextual unlock.

Recommendation

Unlock one section at a time (Model A). It keeps the reward legible — the user knows precisely what one ad bought — and it avoids the "watch several ads to finish one report" pattern this pass explicitly rules out. Session/day unlocks (Model B) read generously but blur into a soft all-access pass, which cannibalizes Premium's core pitch.Unlocking the remainder of the report for the session (Model B) reads generous and reduces repeat-ad friction within one report — but it starts to resemble a temporary all-access pass, which is the thing most likely to cannibalize Premium. Model A (one section per ad) is the safer default; treat B as a variant to test, not the baseline.

09

Frequency & entitlement

Simplest model that still protects Premium

Recommended: a small daily reward allowance — {{ dailyRewardLimit }} rewarded unlocks per day across all features, plus a per-content rule that the same already-unlocked item never asks for a second ad. No per-feature counters beyond that; one global counter is legible to build, explain, and support.

A short cooldown (e.g. the next unlock available in a few minutes, not hours) discourages binge-watching without feeling punitive on an honest single session.
