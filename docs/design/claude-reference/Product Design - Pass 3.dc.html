How Vedic grades a birth-time window
RELIABLE
The window is narrow enough that the nakshatra, its lord and the Dasha balance can't have shifted. Exact dates stand.
APPROXIMATE
The Moon could move within the window but not past a nakshatra boundary — the lord is certain; only the exact balance and dates soften into a range.
UNAVAILABLE
The window crosses a nakshatra boundary, or there is no time at all — the lord itself is uncertain, so no Dasha date is shown. This is Jordan's case.

In plain words

No planets · no houses · no wheel · a table read right to left

Appearances, Android, and what Pass 3 did not touch

Eclipse extended, not redesigned

Light and Dark Eclipse — the rule Pass 3 added
Celestial geometry is always Ink

Natal wheel, Moon, lunation rail: dark in both appearances. A chart wheel on paper reads as a diagram; on ink it reads as the sky. This is the one element that does not invert — everything around it does. 1.03, 2.01.

Vedic and Chinese diagrams invert normally

The square chart, pillars table and element bars are documents, not sky — they take the surface they sit on. Shown on Paper in 3.01 and 4.01, and on Slate in 3.03 and 4.03.

Gilt has two values

#8A6524 for text on Paper, #D9AD63 on Ink and Slate — both carry the same meaning: this part is about you. Cinnabar (#C2472A / #E0603E) is reserved for friction and never used for emphasis.

One typeface added

Noto Serif SC for Chinese characters, at the same optical weight as Cormorant. Nothing else in the type system changed.

Android deltas — only where the platform differs
System sheet

The 5.02 sheet becomes a bottom sheet with a scrim and no corner radius at the top edge. Predictive back gesture returns the previous system without re-rendering Today.

Chart wheel gestures

Long-press replaces 3D-touch peek on a glyph. Tap targets on the wheel stay at 44pt equivalent (48dp), which is why the glyph hit circles are larger than the glyphs.

Ask input

The field docks above the IME rather than floating over the tab bar; the context chips scroll horizontally when the keyboard is up.

Everything else

Identical. The layouts are type and hairlines, so they need no platform-specific treatment.

Entry points left, not designed

Deeper Vedic and BaZi views end in a "more" affordance with no paywall attached. Chart, Sky and Ask all have share affordances that lead nowhere yet. No account, no login, no sync anywhere in Pass 3 — the corrected onboarding is local, and nothing here reintroduces a server. Settings exists only as the system switcher and the voice toggle.
