03

Astrology data safety

The architecture boundary this whole product depends on

Every exact placement, degree, aspect, dasha, nakshatra, house and pillar in every design artifact (including this one) is prototype content. None of it is validated ephemeris output, and none of it should be copied into engineering test fixtures because it appeared in a mock.

Layer 1
Deterministic calculation

Produces validated structured facts: positions, houses, aspects, dashas, pillars, timing. Sole source of truth.

Layer 2
Interpretation

Consumes Layer 1's facts only. Never invents a position, house, aspect, nakshatra, dasha, pillar or timing — Normal and Roast both read from here (§08).

Layer 3
Presentation

Renders Layer 2's output. Design specifies how a value is shown — typography, disclosure, tone — never what the value is.

Exact clock times render only where Layer 1 actually calculated one. Fabricated precision — a time, a degree, a percentage the engine didn't produce — is a defect regardless of which layer introduces it.

Insight → basis, in that order, always

The reading leads with practical meaning; the astrological basis sits one disclosure step beneath it. 08

Roast mode

Same product, same facts — tone only

Normal and Roast consume identical calculated facts and identical interpretation; only expression changes. Roast is playful, dry, sarcastic, concise, never cruel, and punches at a pattern, not a person. It never infers grief, illness, trauma, abuse, breakup, unemployment, crisis or financial hardship, and never attacks body, health, money, identity or protected characteristics.

Normal → Gilt. Roast → Cinnabar, scoped to in-content only. Roast is not a sub-brand: no separate logo, wordmark or icon. Its notification behavior is ruled in §09.

09
Ruling

Roast never reaches a notification

The one contradiction this pass had to resolve outright

SUPERSEDED
Pass 4 · §4.09–4.10

Notification preferences included a live Normal/Roast toggle (toggleNotifRoast) controlling the tone of sample push copy.

GOVERNS
Pass 5.1 · Roast voice rules

"Never in notifications. Roast is opt-in and in-app; it never arrives as a push."

Final rule: Pass 5.1 wins outright — Roast is excluded from notifications entirely. Brand voice is Pass 5.1's domain by the authority map (§05 there), and a push notification is the one surface a joke can't be un-seen or re-framed in: it sits on a lock screen a partner, parent or coworker can glance at, it persists in a notification center without the in-app context that makes "punches a pattern, not the person" legible, and it collides with the brand's own no-urgency/no-FOMO discipline the moment tone becomes a hook to open the app. An opt-in toggle only relocates that risk into a settings row most users never revisit.

Frozen notification-settings behavior

All push notifications ship in Normal register only, regardless of the user's in-app mode. Notification preferences drop the Normal/Roast segmented control from §4.10 entirely — categories only (e.g. daily reading ready, major sky event). A single line of copy in Settings states: "Roast stays in the app — notifications are always Normal."

In-app Roast is unaffected — the user's mode still governs everything they read once they open Humay.

Design tokens, frozen

Color
Ink#12151C — text, dark surface ground. Not a "dark mode" — a surface with meaning (sky, waiting, calculation, Roast).
Paper#F4F1EA — primary light surface for reading.
Gilt#B0863F base · #D9AD63 on Ink · #8A6524 for accent text on Paper (contrast-safe body-size use).

Text hierarchyInk at full, .84, .74, .62, .5, .45 opacity for primary/body/secondary/tertiary/label/disabled — matches every artifact from Pass 2 forward; do not introduce new stops.
Typography
Cormorant GaramondDisplay/headings, weight 300–400. Never bold; the bigger the size, the lighter the weight.
LoraReading body serif, 1.6–1.75 line height. Never used for mono/tabular data.
IBM Plex SansUI copy, labels, controls, kickers (uppercase, tracked ~.04em).
IBM Plex MonoCoordinates, degrees, timestamps, indices — all figures tabular. Mono is never prose.
Dynamic TypeBody scales to 200%. Display caps at 1.4× and re-wraps. Mono caps at 1.2× and falls back to Plex Sans above 1.3× so tables don't shatter.
Spacing / radius / border

Hairline 1px dividers throughout; 2px only for the deliberate "core relationship" emphasis rule. Corners are square by default — Eclipse draws with rules, not rounded containers; the one exception is the occultation mark and widget corner radius (24px), owned by brand.

Shadow / iconography

Shadow restrained to device-frame presentation only, never on in-app surfaces. Icons follow Lucide's line weight and proportions where a glyph is needed; astrological glyphs (planets, signs) are typographic marks, not icon-set icons — see §19 for spoken-label requirements.

Motion

Full detail in §20. Occultation 640ms; screen pushes 320ms; reveal beats ~900ms apart; reduced motion 120–160ms crossfade throughout, nothing loops.

Accessibility handoff

Charts are not accessible only as graphics

Dynamic Type per §14's table · VoiceOver order follows visual reading order (insight → basis, never basis-first) · every zodiac/planet glyph carries a spoken label ("Mercury", "Gemini") rather than reading a Unicode symbol name · contrast holds at body-text minimums against both Ink and Paper grounds · Reduce Motion swaps every signature animation for the crossfade in §20 · Reduce Transparency removes any translucent overlay on gated content.

Hit targets never below 44pt · the natal wheel and Weave diagram both ship a structured-text alternative (a VoiceOver-navigable list of the same placements/dimensions), not just an "image" accessibility label · focus order follows the coordinate rail top-to-bottom, then content · system terminology (Lagna, Nakshatra, Da Yun) gets an explicit pronunciation/label pass with native speakers before ship, not guessed.

Privacy & account assumptions

No login infrastructure is assumed — a slip found and closed

No approved artifact requires authentication. Local profile/birth data, saved people, purchase entitlement (StoreKit, device/receipt-scoped) and ad entitlement (per-item, device-scoped) are four separate concepts — none of them is a login account, and no future sync concept is assumed into existence.

Found in audit — small correction

Pass 3 and Pass 4 copy twice says "account" where it means the local profile ("Sam does not have an account and has not consented…"; the delete-data dialog's "what survives (the account)"). Rename to local profile in both specs — the underlying behavior (nothing survives deletion except what the user explicitly keeps) doesn't change, only the noun. Privacy/data controls (export, delete) remain reachable with no monetization gate, as already specified.

iOS handoff

Behavior notes — no SwiftUI written here

Bottom tab bar with hairline top rule; large-title collapse on scroll for section headers. Safe areas respected on both notch and Dynamic Island devices — the occultation launch animation stays inside the safe area, never behind it. Sheets for transient decisions (§17); context menus on person rows and chart-factor rows (share, remove, deeper view) where they add real value, not decoratively. Haptics: one tick on Mode turn, one on rewarded-unlock success — nowhere else. Dynamic Type per §14. iPhone is the primary layout; iPad adapts the Chart/Compare Systems screens to a two-column split (list + detail) rather than stretching the phone layout. Keyboard: birth-data entry uses native date/time pickers, not a custom keyboard. Share sheet: native, offering the ink share-plate image. Widgets: the four glanceable sizes from Pass 4, no live-activity variant scoped yet. Notifications: local + remote scheduling both possible; content rules per §09 regardless of channel. StoreKit assumptions: Premium is a StoreKit 2 auto-renewable subscription; rewarded-ad entitlement is a local, per-item unlock ledger, not a StoreKit product. Rewarded-ad boundary: the SDK's ad-completion callback is the only trigger for unlock — no client-side "reward" without a verified completion. Accessibility: VoiceOver, Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion are first-class, not audited after the fact.
