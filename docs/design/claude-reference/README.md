## Source-of-truth rule

Astrological facts such as planetary positions, Ascendant, houses, aspects, sidereal placements, nakshatras, dashas, BaZi pillars, and transit timing must ultimately come from deterministic calculation engines. Interpretation/design copy must never become the source of truth for astronomical calculations.

## Naming

"Ortus" appears in some screens (Product Strategy, Pass 2) as a temporary working/design placeholder name — it is not a decided product name. **Astara** is the repository/project name currently in use. Historical screens are not rewritten solely to remove the placeholder name; they are corrected only where the underlying design content itself changed.

## Known unresolved methodology

- **BaZi Da Yun / Luck Pillar methodology**: flagged in Pass 3 as pending. The traditional calculation convention ties the year stem's polarity to the person's sex at birth to determine the forward/reverse direction of the luck pillars — whether and how to collect that input is unresolved.

No other methodology decisions are treated as settled beyond what's shown in the artifacts.
