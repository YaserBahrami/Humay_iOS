# Huma Production Asset Requirements

**Status:** Artwork commission required
**Owner:** Humay visual identity
**Implementation rule:** No generated or hand-authored SwiftUI substitute may ship

## Required deliverables

Produce only two core marks in the first identity delivery:

1. **Primary Huma emblem** — expressive identity use at approximately 64–240 pt.
2. **Compact Huma mark** — an optical simplification of the same bird for approximately 20–48 pt.

A wing, feather, orbital composition, or compatibility variation is not an independent deliverable unless brand review proves a real product use. Any later submark must be derived from the approved bird, not invented as unrelated celestial decoration.

## Character and cultural direction

The Huma must feel mythical, elegant, elevated, graceful, restrained, and recognizably avian. Persian influence should appear through proportion, rhythm, negative space, and refined ornamental restraint rather than historical costume or literal manuscript imitation.

The mark must avoid:

- chicken, eagle-team, phoenix-game, or generic social-media-bird silhouettes;
- sports-logo aggression, mascot facial expression, or children's illustration;
- a circle with two mirrored geometric wings;
- anatomically implausible wing attachment, feather direction, neck, head, or tail;
- arbitrary crowns, eyes, stars, constellations, or orbit lines;
- forced bilateral symmetry that removes flight, posture, and life;
- dense feather detail that collapses below 48 pt;
- AI-like malformed anatomy, accidental tangencies, or noisy Bézier paths.

## Composition requirements

- Bird silhouette must read before internal detail.
- Head, beak, neck, torso, wings, and tail must form one plausible gesture.
- Negative space must remain intentional at both primary and compact sizes.
- Primary mark may use restrained internal feather rhythm; compact mark must simplify rather than merely scale down.
- The emblem must work without an orbit, halo, eye, zodiac sign, or wordmark.
- Relationship to the `humay` wordmark must be optically tested, but the emblem must also stand alone.

## Color and rendering

- First proof must work as one-color black and one-color white.
- Preferred shipping base is a shared transparent vector.
- Gold is an accent option, not mandatory fill and not the only approved treatment.
- Add Light/Dark source variants only if geometry or internal value genuinely changes; do not auto-invert.
- Determine template versus original rendering after one-color and multicolor proofs. Do not force template rendering onto artwork whose internal values carry identity.

## Required size proofs

Review on real backgrounds at these display sizes:

- 20 pt and 24 pt: compact mark only;
- 32 pt and 48 pt: compact and primary comparison;
- 64 pt, 96 pt, 160 pt, and 240 pt: primary emblem;
- iPhone Light and Dark;
- iPad Light and Dark;
- beside the Humay wordmark and alone.

At 20–24 pt, the compact mark must remain recognizably avian without relying on internal feather lines.

## Technical delivery

Deliver:

- editable source file from the production design tool;
- clean SVG source with a stable `viewBox`;
- vector PDF if Xcode rendering verification shows a workflow advantage;
- asset-catalog-ready exports with preserved vector representation;
- documented optical bounds and intended minimum display size;
- source, author, ownership, and license record.

Do not deliver:

- embedded raster images;
- live text, external fonts, or linked resources;
- filters, blur, glow, unsupported blend modes, or rasterizing gradients;
- huge point counts, autogenerated trace noise, redundant clipping masks, or editor metadata;
- separate arbitrary marks that do not share the approved identity construction.

## Acceptance gate

Artwork is not accepted until:

1. avian anatomy and silhouette pass review;
2. Persian-influenced restraint is visible without costume or pastiche;
3. primary and compact marks clearly belong to one identity;
4. one-color and Light/Dark proofs pass;
5. 20–24 pt compact proofs remain legible;
6. SVG/PDF validation finds no rasterization or malformed geometry;
7. Humay owns or has an explicit commercial license for every delivered path;
8. the asset inventory is updated from **production artwork required** to **verified**.

## Temporary product behavior

Phase 1 does not display a fake Huma placeholder. Decorative Huma slots are removed and layouts use typography, whitespace, or the user's initials. Future agents must not recreate the rejected SwiftUI bird while production artwork is pending.
