# D0 Anchor References

D0 defines the composition and appearance relationship for the first three Soulful Celestial screen families.

## Anchor 01 — Today / Home

### Light

- warm cream ground;
- Humay wordmark + celestial sun mark;
- editorial `Your cosmic weather` hero;
- large expressive mood percentage/status;
- one painterly twilight landscape hero framed by orbit lines;
- one illustrated daily-highlight card;
- 2 × 2 Love / Career / Finance / Health cards with distinct but related art palettes;
- compact Planet Mood treatment;
- restrained system navigation.

### Dark

- midnight-navy ground rather than black;
- warm ivory typography;
- hero artwork remains saturated/luminous instead of being globally darkened;
- cards move to deep navy/plum surfaces with lighter hairlines;
- coral/gold/lavender/blue/sage accents are retained and contrast-adjusted;
- decorative stars remain secondary to content.

## Anchor 02 — Daily Horoscope / reading detail

### Light

- editorial headline plus a single moonlit celestial hero;
- small sign/energy chip;
- calm primary reading surface;
- Scorpio/zodiac illustration used as feature art rather than factual chart output;
- same four-category illustration family as Today;
- timing/warning callouts remain compact and subordinate.

### Dark

- night ground integrates naturally with the moonlit hero;
- reading text remains on calm high-contrast surfaces;
- purple/orange category accents stay recognizable;
- no blanket color inversion of illustration.

## Anchor 03 — Compatibility

### Light

- warm human portrait medallions;
- orbit/connection motif between people;
- large score treatment supported by qualitative copy;
- sun/moon hero illustration;
- relationship landscape summary card;
- four dimension cards with distinct symbolic artwork;
- two playful micro-sections (`Who apologizes first?`, `Relationship vibe`) without turning the entire feature into a game.

### Dark

- portrait and relationship art remain colorful focal points;
- midnight surfaces replace cream cards;
- the large score uses a warm luminous accent rather than a neon glow;
- dimension cards retain semantic colors with darker bases.

## Important IA note

The concept masters used temporary navigation labels during visual exploration. They **do not** replace canonical Humay navigation. Production references must use `Today / Circle / Sky / Chart / Ask`, with compatibility reached through Circle.

## Important data note

All percentages, signs, dates, timing windows and reading text in the anchor art are visual fixtures. They are not calculation/methodology authority.

## Master-image handling

The generated raster masters are review artifacts. Before a raster illustration is promoted into the shipping app, extract the underlying reusable art intentionally and export it through the production asset process described in [`../README.md`](../README.md) and [`../../SOULFUL_CELESTIAL_DESIGN_DIRECTION.md`](../../SOULFUL_CELESTIAL_DESIGN_DIRECTION.md).
