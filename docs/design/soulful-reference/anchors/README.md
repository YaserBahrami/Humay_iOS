# D0 Anchor References

D0 is the approved visual source for the current Soulful Celestial direction. The three Light/Dark reference families establish the tone that every later screen must inherit without copying layouts mechanically.

## Anchor 01 — Today / Home

### Light

- warm cream ground with very soft off-white cards;
- large editorial greeting with clean system-body copy;
- small playful sun / steam / sparkle details around the hero rather than across the whole screen;
- one dark cosmic-weather contrast card;
- Love / Career / Money rows use soft tint circles and simple icons;
- Roast Mode gets the strongest playful treatment, but stays compact;
- rounded, quiet bottom navigation.

### Dark

- midnight navy rather than black;
- warm ivory typography;
- coral, lavender and gold remain bright enough to feel alive;
- cards use tonal elevation, thin borders and almost no heavy shadow;
- the hero and Roast illustrations stay colorful instead of being globally darkened;
- dark mode is intentionally composed, not inverted from Light.

## Anchor 02 — Daily Horoscope

### Light

- compact top chrome and date;
- sign identity row with one friendly zodiac illustration;
- quiet segmented Today / Tomorrow / This Week control;
- large serif reading headline inside a calm primary card;
- Love / Career / Money guidance uses restrained category accents;
- one dark Daily Tip card provides contrast and personality.

### Dark

- same hierarchy, rebuilt for midnight surfaces;
- selected controls can use a subtle lavender/coral treatment;
- the zodiac and celestial art stays warm and legible;
- reading copy remains high-contrast and calm.

## Anchor 03 — Compatibility

### Light

- circular portrait medallions connected by a dotted line + heart motif;
- very large serif compatibility score with short qualitative copy;
- four simple dimension rows with thin progress bars;
- one warm `Our Take` summary card with a playful planet illustration;
- generous whitespace keeps the feature premium rather than game-like.

### Dark

- portrait circles remain colorful focal points;
- score can use a warm coral/lavender emphasis without neon glow;
- metric bars retain semantic color against dark neutral tracks;
- the relationship summary card gets a slightly warmer raised surface.

## Visual rules carried forward

- editorial serif for emotional hierarchy; system typography for utility;
- simple flat/vector or thin-line illustration with very limited facial expressions;
- cream / midnight foundation with disciplined coral, lavender, gold, blue and sage accents;
- feature cards generally 18–24 pt radius;
- sparse stars, sparkles, orbit lines and dotted connectors;
- fun in illustration and copy, not through dense decoration;
- no watercolor, painterly landscapes, 3D emoji, chibi, thick cartoon outlines, neon galaxy backgrounds or default glassmorphism.

## Important IA note

The concept masters used temporary navigation labels during visual exploration. They **do not** replace canonical Humay navigation. Production IA remains `Today / Circle / Sky / Chart / Ask`, with Compatibility reached through Circle and Profile/Settings through the profile entry.

## Important data note

All percentages, signs, dates, scores, timing windows and reading text in the anchor art are visual fixtures unless separately validated by Humay's calculation/methodology layer.

## Master-image handling

The committed anchor images are design references. Reusable shipping artwork must be extracted intentionally, optimized, and exported into `Humay/Resources/Assets.xcassets/` only after approval.
